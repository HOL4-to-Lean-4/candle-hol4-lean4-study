# Fidelity audit of a HOL4-to-Lean 4 translation (Candle `proves_sound` closure)

A model translated a reduced HOL4 development (CakeML's Candle kernel soundness theorem
`holSoundness.proves_sound`) into Lean 4.28.0 (core + Batteries, no Mathlib): all definitions,
datatypes, inductive relations and HOL4 library constants, plus 278 theorem statements with
`sorry` proofs. It compiles, and a kernel check confirms the 278 theorems exist and only they use
`sorry`. Your job is to find **semantic** differences between the Lean and the HOL4: places where
a Lean definition or statement means something different from the HOL4 one on some input, or a
statement is weaker, stronger, false, vacuous or trivially true when the HOL4 one is not.

Earlier translations of CakeML had real defects of these kinds, so check them on purpose:
integer/natural division and modulus (HOL4 `DIV`/`MOD`/`/`/`%` conventions vs Lean), truncated
subtraction, characters (HOL4 `char` is 8-bit) and strings, derived `BEq`/`DecidableEq` used in
place of HOL equality, finite maps (`fmap`) and finiteness, sets that may be infinite, words and
bit widths, Hilbert choice and `ARB`, under-specified functions (`HD []`), recursion that HOL4
accepts with a different termination measure or different pattern-matching order (overlapping
patterns: HOL4 takes the first match), type variables and their instantiation (an extra
`[Nonempty α]` or a too-specific type), overload resolution (for example set-theory `pair` vs HOL
pairs, `mem` argument), operator precedence and associativity, quantifier scope, implicit
universal quantification of free variables, `⇔`/`=` on booleans, inductive relations (rules,
least fixed point), and statements whose HOL4 text is generated by ML code (`|> SIMP_CONV`,
`CONJUNCTS`, `GEN_ALL`) that the translator had to work out by hand.
